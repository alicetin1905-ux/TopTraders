Methodology. For each fixture, away-win likelihood is the average of the home team's home-loss rate and the away team's away-win rate over their recent matches; BTTS likelihood is the average of the home team's home-BTTS rate and the away team's away-BTTS rate. Combined multiplies the two, treating them as independent — a simplification, not a true joint probability. Fixtures where either team has fewer than 5 recent matches on record are flagged "low sample".
